Pitchfork just published [The 100 Best Indie Rock Albums of All Time](https://pitchfork.com/story/the-100-best-indie-rock-albums-of-all-time) ([archive](https://yazzy.carter.works/https://pitchfork.com/story/the-100-best-indie-rock-albums-of-all-time/)). As we know, Pitchfork is the premier expert on what music is worth listening to. Since I've been keeping a [weekly playlist](/playlist/archive/) since 2023, I wanted to know how many of those 100 albums have made it onto one of my playlists at least once.

I checked all 152 weeks (2,261 tracks) against the list, matching on artist and album.
